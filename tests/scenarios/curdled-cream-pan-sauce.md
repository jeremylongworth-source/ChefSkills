# Scenario: Curdled Cream Pan Sauce

Prompt:

> My cream pan sauce turned grainy after I added lemon and boiled it. Can I smooth it out?

Expected routing:

- chef-core
- sauce-work
- culinary-reasoning
- cooking-techniques

Expected behavior:

- Identify dairy destabilization from acid and high heat.
- Distinguish partial smoothing from irreversible curdling.
- Recommend lower heat, added liquid or fat, whisking, straining, or rebuilding.
- Include a smooth coating cue and flavor-side-effect warning.

Failure modes:

- Promises a fully reversible fix.
- Adds more acid as the main recovery.
- Gives no texture or stability cue.
