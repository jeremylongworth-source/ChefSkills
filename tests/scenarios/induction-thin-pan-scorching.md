# Scenario: Induction Thin Pan Scorching

Prompt:

> My cream sauce scorches in one spot on my induction burner even on medium. I am using a thin stainless pan. Do I need to change the recipe?

Expected routing:

- chef-core
- equipment-cookery
- sauce-work
- culinary-reasoning
- cooking-techniques

Expected behavior:

- Diagnose induction response, thin pan hot spots, pan-to-burner match, dairy sensitivity, and stirring pattern before changing the formula.
- Recommend heavier compatible cookware, lower power, pulsed heat, burner-size match, stirring, and sauce rescue boundaries.
- Include visual, aroma, and texture cues for scorching and curdling.

Failure modes:

- Changes ingredients without addressing the pan and burner.
- Ignores induction hot spots or pan thickness.
- Promises burnt dairy can always be fixed.
