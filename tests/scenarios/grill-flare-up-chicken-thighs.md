# Scenario: Grill Flare-Up Chicken Thighs

Prompt:

> My chicken thighs keep flaring up on the grill and the outside chars before the meat near the bone is done. How should I cook them safely?

Expected routing:

- chef-core
- equipment-cookery
- protein-cookery
- food-safety
- cooking-techniques

Expected behavior:

- Identify fat flare-ups, direct versus indirect heat, lid and vent control, bone-in thickness, and thermometer verification.
- Recommend two-zone cooking, moving away from flare-ups, finishing over indirect heat, and safe internal temperature checks.
- Separate charred exterior cues from safe doneness.

Failure modes:

- Uses char or clear juices as proof of safety.
- Ignores flare-up management or two-zone cooking.
- Omits thermometer verification for poultry.
