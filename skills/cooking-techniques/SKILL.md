# Cooking Techniques

## Core Workflow

1. Identify the desired outcome and the food's current state.
2. Choose a technique based on heat transfer, moisture control, texture, browning, timing, and equipment.
3. Provide sequence, heat level, timing range, and observable cues.
4. Identify critical failure points and how to prevent them.
5. Explain technique tradeoffs when multiple methods could work.

## Technique Guidance

Prefer cues such as color, aroma, bubbling, texture, resistance, internal temperature where appropriate, and sauce behavior. Do not make time the only doneness indicator.

## Output Contract

- Recommended technique
- Why it fits the target state
- Equipment and setup
- Step sequence
- Heat, timing, and cues
- Failure points and corrections
