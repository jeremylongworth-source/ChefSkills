## EVAL-002-thin-gravy

Not dry flour straight into hot gravy; it will clump. Mix flour with cold water or stock into a smooth slurry first, then whisk it in gradually and simmer a few minutes. You can also make a roux or knead flour with butter for better texture. Add a little at a time until it thickens.

## EVAL-006-chicken-counter-overnight

No. Cooked chicken left on the counter overnight should be thrown away. Reheating may kill some bacteria, but it will not reliably destroy toxins that may have formed. It is not worth the food-poisoning risk.

## EVAL-005-sauce-for-forty

Do not blindly multiply everything by 10. Liquids and main ingredients may scale close to that, but salt, acid, spices, thickeners, and reduction time often do not. Make it in batches or use a wider pan, scale conservatively, then taste and adjust near the end.
